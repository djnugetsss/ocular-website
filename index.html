Privacy

Nothing to upload.
Nothing to trust us with.

Ocular was built so that the sensitive part — your face — never becomes data anyone else can hold. Not us. Not a server. Not an advertiser.

  • Camera frames never leave the phone

    Video is analysed in memory, frame by frame, and discarded immediately. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or stored.

  • Entirely on-device

    All processing runs locally through Apple's Vision framework and the Neural Engine. Ocular works in airplane mode.

  • No ads

    No banners, no sponsored content, no attention economy. The app has one job and it does that job.

  • No trackers

    No third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app profiling.

  • Only derived metrics are saved

    What persists is a handful of numbers — blink rate, steadiness, session length — kept locally on your iPhone.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Ocular record or upload video?

No. Frames from the front camera are analysed in memory and released straight away. There is no recording, no upload, and no server that could receive one. Only derived numbers — like blink rate and steadiness — are written to your device.

How does the measurement actually work?

Ocular uses Apple's Vision framework to detect face landmarks and head pose on-device. Eyelid geometry over time gives blink events; yaw, pitch and roll variance over the session gives head steadiness. Everything runs on the Neural Engine, so it's fast and battery-friendly.

Is Ocular a medical device?

No. Ocular is a wellness app for general awareness of screen habits. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. If your eyes hurt, your vision changes, or symptoms persist, please talk to an eye care professional.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Because every measurement happens locally, Ocular is fully functional offline — including in airplane mode.

Which devices are supported?

Ocular is designed for iPhone running iOS 17 or later, with a front-facing camera. We'll confirm the exact device list on the App Store listing at launch.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It's gone. Your sessions live in the app's local storage, so deleting Ocular removes them with it. You can also clear your history from inside the app at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.
