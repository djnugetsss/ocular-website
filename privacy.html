In short: the camera feed is analysed on your iPhone and immediately discarded. No photos or video are ever recorded, stored, or uploaded. What leaves your device is a small set of numeric results — things like blink rate and a steadiness score — synced to your private account so your history follows you. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell your data.

1. Summary #

Ocular is an eye wellness app for iPhone. It uses Apple's Vision framework to measure blink awareness and head steadiness while you sit in front of your phone, and turns those measurements into trends you can look back on.

Two ideas shape how the app treats your data. First, the camera feed is the most sensitive thing the app touches, so it never becomes stored data — not on our systems, not in your photo library, not anywhere. Second, the results of a session are yours, so they are tied to an account you control and can export or erase at any time.

2. Scope #

This policy describes how the Ocular iOS app (“Ocular”, “the app”) and the Ocular website handle information. It applies to everyone who downloads the app, creates an account, or visits this site.

In this document, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of Ocular. Ocular is an independent app; contact details are in Section 16.

3. Information we collect #

Account information

Ocular uses Supabase Auth for sign-in. When you create an account, we store the identifier you sign in with — typically an email address — together with an account ID and the dates your account was created and last updated. Passwords, where used, are stored only as salted hashes by the authentication service and are never visible to us.

Session metrics

When a check-in finishes, the app saves the numeric results of that session. These are the derived outputs of the measurement, not the measurement input. They include:

  • Blink rate
  • Blink consistency
  • Head steadiness score
  • Session duration
  • Timestamps for when the session started and ended

These records are associated with your account so your history and trends are available to you.

Preferences

Settings you choose inside the app — such as reminder options, units, and display preferences — are stored with your account so the app behaves consistently for you.

Subscription status

If you purchase a subscription, Apple tells the app whether an entitlement is active. We store only that status. We never receive your payment card number, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

Support correspondence

If you email us for support, we receive your message, your email address, and anything else you choose to include. We keep support email only as long as needed to resolve the issue and maintain a reasonable record of it.

4. Information we do not collect #

To be explicit about the categories people most often ask about, Ocular does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to:

  • Photos or video. No frame from the camera is ever written to storage or transmitted off the device.
  • Face templates or biometric identifiers. Ocular measures movement over time, not identity. It cannot recognise who you are, and it does not create or retain a faceprint.
  • Audio. The app does not use the microphone.
  • Location data. The app does not request or use location services.
  • Contacts, calendars, photo library, or health records. None of these are requested.
  • Advertising identifiers. Ocular does not use the IDFA and does not participate in cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Third-party analytics or attribution SDKs. There are none in the app.
  • Payment details. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

5. Camera usage #

Ocular requests access to the front-facing camera for one purpose: to measure blink activity and head position while a check-in is running.

  • Frames are held in memory only for as long as it takes to analyse them, then released. They are not written to disk, not added to your photo library, and not transmitted.
  • Nothing is recorded. There is no video file, no still image, and no buffer that persists after a session ends.
  • The camera is active only during an active check-in. iOS shows its standard camera indicator whenever it is in use, so you can always verify this independently of anything we say here.

You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera. Measurement will not work without it, but revoking access does not delete sessions you have already saved.

6. On-device processing #

All image analysis happens on your iPhone, using Apple's Vision framework and the device's own hardware. Face landmark detection and head pose estimation run locally; the app derives blink events from eyelid geometry over time and a steadiness score from variation in head orientation across the session.

No image data is sent anywhere for processing. There is no server-side vision pipeline, no remote inference, and no “cloud enhancement” step. The only thing that can leave your device is the numeric result described in Section 3.

Because measurement is local, a check-in itself works without a network connection. Signing in and syncing completed sessions do require connectivity; sessions recorded offline sync once your device is back online.

7. How we use information #

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Save your sessions and show you your history, trends, and insights.
  • Apply your preferences across the app.
  • Unlock features you have subscribed to.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
  • Diagnose and fix problems, and protect against abuse or misuse of the service.
  • Comply with legal obligations that apply to us.

We do not use your session metrics for advertising, and we do not use them to train models for third parties.

8. Supabase and service providers #

Ocular uses Supabase as its backend for two things: authentication (Supabase Auth) and storage of the account data and session metrics described in Section 3. Supabase acts as a processor on our behalf — it hosts the data so we can provide the service, and it is not permitted to use it for its own purposes.

Access to that data is constrained by Postgres Row Level Security. Policies are written so that a signed-in user can read and write only rows belonging to their own account; a request carrying one user's credentials cannot return another user's sessions.

Apple is also involved, in its own right rather than as our processor: it distributes the app and processes any purchase you make. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any other third-party processor for personal information. If that changes, this section will be updated before the change takes effect.

9. Data retention #

We keep information only as long as it serves the purpose it was collected for:

  • Session metrics are retained until you delete them or delete your account. There is no automatic expiry, because the value of the app is the trend over time — that choice stays with you.
  • Account information is retained for the life of your account.
  • Camera frames are retained for the duration of the analysis and no longer. They are never persisted, so there is nothing to expire.
  • Support email is retained while your request is open and for a reasonable period afterwards.

After you delete your account, residual copies may persist briefly in encrypted infrastructure backups. Those backups age out on a rolling schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.

10. Exporting and deleting data #

Ocular gives you direct control from inside the app, without needing to email anyone:

  • Export your data. You can export your sessions in a machine-readable format and keep or move that copy wherever you like.
  • Delete an individual session. Any single check-in can be removed on its own.
  • Delete all sessions. You can clear your entire measurement history while keeping your account.
  • Delete your account. See Section 11.

Deletions apply to the stored records themselves, not merely to what is displayed in the app.

11. Account deletion #

You can permanently delete your Ocular account from within the app. Deleting your account removes your authentication record, your session metrics, and your stored preferences.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want to keep your history, export it first (Section 10). Deleting your account does not by itself cancel an active subscription — subscriptions are managed by Apple, and you should cancel through your Apple Account settings as described in the Terms of Use.

If you are unable to complete deletion in the app for any reason, email oculapp@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will process the request.

12. Security practices #

We take a layered approach appropriate to the small amount of data the app holds:

  • Minimisation. The most sensitive input — the camera feed — is never stored, so it cannot be breached, subpoenaed, or leaked.
  • Keychain storage. Session credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain, which is protected by the device's hardware-backed encryption and passcode.
  • Encryption in transit. All communication between the app and the backend uses TLS.
  • Encryption at rest. Data stored in the backend is encrypted at rest by the hosting provider.
  • Row Level Security. Database policies enforce per-user isolation at the data layer, not merely in application code.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users and any regulator we are required to inform, without undue delay.

13. Children's privacy #

Ocular is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Creating an account requires you to be at least 13 years old, as set out in the Terms of Use.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email oculapp@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data.

14. International users #

Ocular is operated from the United States, and the infrastructure that stores account data and session metrics may be located in the United States or in other countries where our hosting provider operates. If you use the app from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in those countries, which may have different data protection rules than your own.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. The export and deletion tools described in Section 10 and Section 11 let you exercise the access, portability, and erasure rights directly; for anything else, email oculapp@gmail.com.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to perform our agreement with you (providing the app), on the basis of your consent for camera access, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working. Where the CCPA applies, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

15. Changes to this policy #

We may update this policy as the app changes. When we do, we will revise the “last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will give notice in the app before it takes effect, and where the law requires it, ask for your consent.

Continuing to use Ocular after an update means you accept the revised policy.

16. Contact #

Questions about this policy, or about your data, can be sent to oculapp@gmail.com. Please write from the email address associated with your account where possible — it helps us verify a request before acting on it.

We aim to respond to privacy requests within 30 days.
