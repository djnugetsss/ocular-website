Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version: camera frames never leave your iPhone, all analysis happens on-device, there are no ads or third-party trackers, and the only things saved are derived metrics stored locally on your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Ocular iPhone app (“Ocular”, “the app”) and this website handle information. It applies to everyone who uses the app or visits this site.

Ocular is designed on a simple principle: the most sensitive input the app touches — the live camera feed of your face — should never become data that anyone, including us, is able to hold.

2. Camera use

Ocular asks for access to the front-facing camera so it can measure blink activity and head position during a check-in.

  • Frames are processed in memory and immediately discarded. They are never written to disk, never added to your photo library, and never transmitted.
  • No video or still images are recorded by the app at any point.
  • No face templates or biometric identifiers are created or stored. Ocular measures movement, not identity — it cannot recognise who you are.
  • The camera is active only while a check-in is running, and iOS displays its standard camera indicator whenever it is.

You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Ocular's measurement features require it, but revoking access does not delete anything you have already saved.

3. On-device processing

All analysis runs locally using Apple's Vision framework and the on-device Neural Engine. Ocular does not send camera data, face data, or measurement data to any server — ours or anyone else's. The app is fully functional without a network connection, including in airplane mode.

4. What is stored, and where

After a check-in, Ocular saves a small set of derived metrics so you can see your history and trends. These typically include:

  • Blink count and blink rate for the session
  • A head steadiness score
  • Session start time and duration
  • Your in-app settings and preferences

These records are written to Ocular's private storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded, not synced to a server, and not shared with us. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, app data may be included in your own encrypted device backup — that backup is controlled by you and by Apple, not by Ocular.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not serve ads or work with advertising networks.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with data brokers.
  • We do not build user profiles or maintain accounts — Ocular has no sign-up.

6. Purchases

If Ocular offers a paid tier, purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether an entitlement is active. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Diagnostics

If you choose to share crash and usage diagnostics with developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying crash reports. This is an Apple-operated system, it is off unless you opt in, and it never includes camera data or your session metrics.

8. This website

This site is a static marketing page. It sets no cookies, embeds no tracking pixels, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested URL) for security and reliability, as is normal for any web host.

9. Children

Ocular is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but if you have a concern, please contact us.

10. Your rights and control

Because your data stays on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Clear your session history from within the app at any time.
  • Revoke camera access in iOS Settings.
  • Delete Ocular — removing the app removes its stored metrics with it.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA to access, correct, or delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold none — there is no server-side record of you to produce or erase.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes that affect how data is handled will also be surfaced in the app before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@ocular.app and we will get back to you.
